THE GUIDE

DANISH WINTER SCENT.

It’s −2°C, gray and short on sun. Your perfume has to fight back. These are the affordable bottles you can grab at Normal, Bilka or Matas that actually hold up when the wind is coming off the harbour.

06 SCENTS / UNDER 600 KR EACH / NO GUESSWORK
THE COLD-RULES.
01

GO WARM

Cold air kills fresh citrus before it reaches anyone’s nose. You need vanilla, amber, spice or wood — notes with mass.

02

GO THICK

At 3–8°C you need projection. Eau de parfum or extrait, sprayed on skin over coat, collar or scarf.

03

GO SWEET

Dark, slightly sweet scents read as “warm person” in Denmark. That is a social advantage. Use it.

04

BUY SMART

Every pick below sits in regular drugstores and department stores. Sale season in November + January cuts them hard.

HOW TO WEAR IT. IN DENMARK.
A

Inside of the coat. The collar and inner lapel. Perfume on a coat stays warm against your body and releases slowly the whole evening — and your coat is on for 22 hours a day.

B

Wet skin first. A little unscented lotion, then the perfume. Dry winter skin eats scent alive; moisturized skin keeps it going for 8+ hours.

C

Don’t layer two. Pick one and commit. Danish cold is honest — a single strong scent reads better than two that fight each other.

D

Buy in the sale. November and January, Bilka and Normal cut the bottles above by 30–50%. That is when Eros and Ultra Male become almost embarrassingly cheap.
